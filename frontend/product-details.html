    Premium Tournament Construction

    Every Prosix sports uniform is precision-engineered to meet the rigorous demands of professional athletes:

    • Fabric: 180-220 GSM heavyweight Poly-Spandex with dual-action antimicrobial moisture-wicking technology.
    • Sublimation: Italian dye-sublimation ink infused directly into polymer fibers at 400°F for zero peeling or fading.
    • Athletic Cut: Ergonomically curved raglan sleeves and multi-directional stretch for unrestricted motion.
    • Reinforced Seams: 6-thread flatlock ergonomic stitching built for high-contact athletics.
