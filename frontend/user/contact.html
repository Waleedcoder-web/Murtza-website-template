    About Snowtop Sports

    Snowtop Sports is a premier manufacturer and international exporter of high-performance custom sports uniforms, athletic apparel, compression gear, and tournament teamwear. Headquartered in Sialkot, Pakistan — the world’s renowned capital for sporting goods manufacturing — we engineer championship-grade apparel for professional clubs, academies, collegiate teams, athletic brands, and gyms across the United States, United Kingdom, Europe, Canada, and Australia.

    Our comprehensive in-house manufacturing infrastructure covers everything from high-resolution digital dye-sublimation with premium anti-fade Italian inks, precision laser-cutting, to heavy-duty 6-thread ergonomic flatlock seam construction. By cutting out brokers and retail distributors, Snowtop Sports delivers factory-direct wholesale pricing with unbeatable turnaround times and zero compromise on fabric durability.
