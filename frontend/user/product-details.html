    How to Measure

    Use a soft tape measure. Keep it level and snug, but not tight.

    • 1Chest: around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms.
    • 2Waist: around your natural waist, the narrowest point.
    • 3Hips: around the fullest part of the hips, feet together.
    • 4Underbust: around the ribcage, just under the bust.
    • 5Inseam: from the top of the inner leg down to the ankle bone.
    • 6Height: standing straight against a wall, without shoes.
    • 7Head: around the head, about 2 cm above the eyebrows and ears.
    Fit Recommendation: If you're between two sizes, or right on the line, go by the chest for tops and by the hips for shorts, pants and leggings. Size up if you prefer a looser fit.
